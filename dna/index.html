How does it work?

Once you have paid, a courier
will deliver an agreement
and a kit for collecting
a DNA sample
from saliva - a simple
and painless process

Specialists at the laboratory
Gordiz extract the DNA,
check its purity
and concentration,
preserve it and apply it
to a special sterile thread

The thread is placed
inside an airtight
glass pendant.
The air inside is replaced
with argon, an inert gas.
